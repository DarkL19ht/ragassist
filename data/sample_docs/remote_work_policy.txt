RAGASSIST SAMPLE COMPANY
REMOTE WORK POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy explains the remote-working arrangements
for employees of RAGAssist Sample Company.

2. Eligibility

Employees may request remote-working arrangements
after completing three months of employment.

Remote-working requests must be approved by the
employee's line manager.

3. Working Hours

Employees are expected to remain available during
their agreed core working hours of 10:00 to 15:00,
Monday to Friday.

4. Working From Abroad

Employees must obtain written approval from HR
before working from another country.

Approval depends on employment, tax, security,
and operational requirements.

5. Equipment

The company provides a work laptop to employees
approved for remote work.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure
internet connection.

6. Data Security

Employees must use approved company systems
and must not store confidential company data
on personal devices.

7. Review

Remote-working arrangements are reviewed
every six months.
